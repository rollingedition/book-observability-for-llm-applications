# Playing a song from your library

Name the song and, if it helps, the artist. The assistant looks in your music library first and plays the first song that matches. If more than one song has the name, it lists the artists and asks which one you mean. A song that is not in your library cannot be played, and the assistant says so rather than choosing another. Songs play through the device you chose last, unless you name another. The assistant reports the title and the artist when a song starts.
