# Direct messages and social posts

A direct message goes to one person in a social service, and a post goes to everyone who follows you. Ask the assistant to send a direct message by naming the person as they appear on that service. To post, say what the post should say; the assistant shows you the text and the length before it posts. Posts longer than the service allows are shortened only if you agree. The assistant can read back the latest direct messages, but it cannot see messages sent in a private group you have left.
