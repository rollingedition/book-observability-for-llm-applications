# Adding a smart plug

A smart plug switches the device plugged into it. To add one, put the plug in a socket near your router, then open the device list and choose to add a plug. The assistant asks for a name, such as "desk lamp" or "kettle", and for the room it is in. Keep the plug within range of your home network while it connects. When the plug shows as online, the assistant confirms it. A plug added this way can then be switched on and off by name. Plugs do not report how much power they use unless the plug supports it, and the assistant says so if you ask.
