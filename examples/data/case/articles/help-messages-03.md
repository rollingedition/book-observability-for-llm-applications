# Writing a short message

Say who the message is for and what it should say. Keep it to a few sentences. The assistant reads the names back before it sends anything, so you can correct a name you did not mean. Subject lines are optional; if you leave one out, the assistant writes a short one from the first sentence. The message is sent only after you confirm it. Once it is sent, it cannot be recalled by the assistant, so check the wording first. Long messages are split into parts only in the rare case where the service limits their length, and the assistant tells you when that happens.
