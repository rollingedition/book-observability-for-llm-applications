# Checking whether a device is online

Ask whether a device is online and the assistant reports its status: online, offline, or updating. A device is offline when it has lost its connection to the home network. Check that the device has power, that it is in range of the network, and that the network is working for other devices. An updating device may be unavailable for several minutes. The assistant reports the last time the device was seen, so you can tell whether it has been offline for a while or only for a moment.
