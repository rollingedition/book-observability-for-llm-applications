# Finding music by genre

Name a genre, such as jazz, folk or classical, and the assistant plays music from that genre in your library. If your library has none of that genre, the assistant says so and offers to play a radio station instead. Genres come from the tags on each song, so a song with no tag is not found by genre. To make a playlist from a genre, name the genre and the number of songs you want, and the assistant lists the songs before saving.
