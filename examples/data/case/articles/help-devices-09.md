# Turning a speaker on and off

Speakers can be switched on and off by name. Turning a speaker off stops any sound it is making, and turning it on does not start anything by itself. To set the level, ask for a volume from zero to one hundred. Speakers in a group change together. A speaker that is offline cannot be switched, and the assistant says so. Speakers can also be set to turn on at a time you choose, with the volume they should start at.
