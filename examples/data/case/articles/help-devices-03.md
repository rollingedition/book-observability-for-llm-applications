# Turning lights on and off

Name the light, or the room, and say whether to turn it on or off. The assistant changes the light and confirms it. To turn off every light in a room at once, say "all lights in the room". Lights that are not reachable are reported as offline, and the assistant does not change them. Some bulbs take a few seconds to respond, so the assistant may report the change a moment after you ask. Lights can also be set to turn on or off at a time you choose; the assistant shows the schedule before it saves it.
