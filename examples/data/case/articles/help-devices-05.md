# Starting a robot vacuum

A robot vacuum can clean now or at a time you set. To start it, name the vacuum and say which room to clean, or say whole house. The vacuum must be on its dock or on the floor with a clear path. If the vacuum reports that it is stuck, the assistant tells you where it is and suggests moving the obstacle. A cleaning run can be paused and resumed, and it returns to its dock when finished. Cleaning schedules are shown in full before they are saved, with the days and the times.
