# Naming a device

Each device has a name and a room. Names should be short and unique within the room, such as "left lamp" or "bedroom fan". To rename a device, say the old name and the new one. The assistant checks that no other device in the same room has the new name. Renaming a device does not change its settings, its schedules or its history. If two devices have the same name in different rooms, say the room as well when you give a command, so the assistant knows which one you mean.
