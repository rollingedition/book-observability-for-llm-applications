# Following a podcast

Follow a podcast to be told when a new episode is released. Ask the assistant to follow a show by name. Episodes play in the order they were released, or the latest first, as you choose. The assistant can play the latest episode, or a named one. Episodes you have started are remembered, so you can resume them later. Unfollowing a show keeps its episodes you have already listened to, but removes it from future reports of new episodes.
