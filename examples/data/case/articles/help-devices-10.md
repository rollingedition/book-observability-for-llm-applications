# Restarting a device that does not respond

When a device stops responding, first check that it has power and that the network is working. Then ask the assistant to restart the device. The restart takes about a minute, and the assistant reports when the device is back online. Settings and names are kept through a restart. If the device still does not respond, the assistant suggests removing it and adding it again, from the start of the setup. Removing a device deletes its schedules, so the assistant asks you to confirm first.
